# Ceramic portrait stands — local implementation

Three original painted-ceramic head stands are available in the existing RU/EN appearance control: Alexander Pushkin, Ernest Hemingway and Leo Tolstoy. Connected head, hair and beard surfaces replace the rejected cape-like forms. Heads include ears, eyelids, irises and individual facial proportions; the surface uses authored color and a shared owned reflection per stand. No portrait photographs are used as runtime textures.

The owner's final dimensional correction makes the lower white saucer one third narrower than the preceding doubled version: profile radius 0.590 × 2/3 = 0.393333, diameter 0.786667, depth scale 0.94. The rounded raised rim and recessed well flow into the neck. Heads and attached details retain their earlier uniform 12% enlargement about the upper contact point; the globe contact and overall bottom height stay fixed. These are local model units, not physical manufacturing dimensions.

The three explicit owner additions are self-canonical included items, separate from the immutable 29 mandatory Starter Set requirements. They do not introduce store SKUs or child approval. The seven composition owners, one scene/canvas, antique map, golden whales and 984-book library are retained. Preview, Apply, Cancel, RU/EN labels, all quality tiers and whole-composition persistence use the existing pipeline.

## Evidence and limits

- Final source-bound checks and exact artifacts are recorded in result.json; attempts.json preserves failed and superseded runs without relabelling them as current.
- art-history.json preserves captures and actual reviewer attribution. The final isolated inspection covers High front/profile; actual-App captures cover all three High and Economy choices plus a narrow viewport.
- Earlier size passes are superseded by successive explicit owner corrections. Capture framing defects are recorded separately from runtime geometry defects. No historical reports are overwritten.
- The normal fix gives thin closed eye rims separate shading vertices. The initial browser attempt failed before app execution because a fixture constant was missing; the fixture was repaired.
- Only four new ceramic unit cases and two exact adapter allowlist cases are selected. Other adapter cases are excluded, not counted as passing. TypeScript and one focused actual-App scenario accompany local PWA/Android builds.

This is a working local sculptural implementation, not accepted portrait likeness or the requested maximum realism. Faces remain generalized, eyes too doll-like, Pushkin's curls regular, Tolstoy's beard too parallel/fluted, and the existing warm/violet lighting strongly colors the ivory glaze. Exact selected mature Hemingway and Repin portrait pixels were not inspected through the available remote tools; source metadata is not visual approval. See reference-notes.md for provenance and limits. These limitations remain work to be done.
