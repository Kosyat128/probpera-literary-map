## 5. Claims rules

Every number is generated from RC reports.
Do not say:

- best;
- number one;
- official;
- complete world archive;
- all books;
- guaranteed development;
- Disney available;
- AI-free in absolute terms unless all relevant production pipelines are
  audited and wording is legally approved.

May say, if proven:

- verified biographies;
- real approved portraits;
- no advertising;
- no subscription;
- one-time paid app;
- optional additions;
- child mode included;
- offline content available.
