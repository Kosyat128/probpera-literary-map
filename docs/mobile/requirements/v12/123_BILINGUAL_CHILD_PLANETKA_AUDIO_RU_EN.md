# BILINGUAL CHILD MODE, PLANETKA AND AUDIO / ДЕТСКИЙ РЕЖИМ И ПЛАНЕТКА

## 1. Equal safety

English child mode must be as complete and restrictive as Russian child
mode. Language is applied after identity/age policy, never before policy.

## 2. Planetka identity

Russian name: `Планетка`.
English name: `Planetka`.

English first introduction may say:

> “I’m Planetka, your guide to stories, writers and places around the
> world.”

Do not rename the character on different screens.

## 3. Dialogue adaptation

Planetka English dialogue:

- natural spoken English;
- age-banded alternatives;
- short sentences for younger children;
- terminology from the glossary;
- no invented facts;
- no urgency, scarcity or purchase pressure;
- no open generative chat;
- no request for child personal data;
- explicit offline/error scripts;
- reduced-motion alternatives.

## 4. Child language controls

- parent chooses allowed profile languages;
- child may switch between allowed languages without Parent Gate unless
  parent locks it;
- language switch cannot expose adult history, search or store;
- child profile progress is language-neutral;
- reading/narration progress maps to the same entity ID;
- age decisions are shared, while wording is locale-specific.

## 5. Reading levels

English child content uses documented bands such as:

- ages 3–5: icon-led, short narrated phrases;
- ages 6–8: early-reader explanations;
- ages 9–12: concise biographies and contextual vocabulary;
- ages 13–15: richer literary context;
- ages 16–17: extended child-safe archive.

Do not substitute a numeric readability score for editorial age approval.

## 6. Audio

For every English audio item store:

- script ID and source hash;
- voice performer/licensor;
- pronunciation notes;
- locale/accent;
- duration;
- transcript/subtitles;
- age range;
- rights/territories/platforms;
- loudness/quality report;
- reduced-audio fallback.

Neutral clear English is preferred. Regional accents are allowed when
intentional and documented. Do not clone celebrity, actor, Disney or real
writer voices.

## 7. Pronunciation

Maintain pronunciation dictionaries for writer names, places and work
titles. Provide editor preview and phonetic notes; do not change displayed
spelling to solve narration.

## 8. Offline

The included English child pack contains:

- Planetka core dialogue;
- onboarding;
- selected journeys;
- age-safe writer/work text;
- transcripts;
- essential audio at an approved quality tier;
- error/help messages.

Audio download status is separate per locale. Missing English audio must
fall back to English text, not Russian speech.

## 9. Accessibility

- captions/transcripts for all narration;
- VoiceOver/TalkBack labels in English;
- no information conveyed only by voice;
- controls announce current language;
- speech can be muted independently;
- no autoplay conflict with system accessibility preferences.

## 10. Child QA

Run every bypass, exact-age, offline, purchase and Parent Gate case in both
languages. English alias search and English deep links must not bypass the
same policy that blocks Russian access.
