Ребёнок не видит price, Buy CTA, discount, paid ranking или licensed promo.
Видит только included либо уже owned child-safe items.

ноги; face/limbs separate from geographical surface; reduced motion;
география не искажается.

======================================================================
H. TRUE 3D CUSTOMIZATION
======================================================================

Скины меняют поверхность того же глобуса. Stands and backgrounds —
независимые scene layers. Нельзя remount Canvas или сбрасывать camera,
selected country/writer и semantic state.

Premium backgrounds High/Balanced должны быть настоящими 3D-сценами с:

- geometry;
- depth/parallax;
- PBR materials;
- lighting/light probes/lightmaps where applicable;
- LOD;
- spatially coherent particles/animation;
- memory budgets;
- Economy/static fallback;
- accessibility/reduced motion;
- rights/provenance.

2.5D/static cannot be sold as full 3D. Background switching is
transactional: preload → integrity/compatibility/memory check → shader
warmup → controlled transition → commit → old resource disposal; error
keeps previous composition. Rapid A→B→C commits only C.
