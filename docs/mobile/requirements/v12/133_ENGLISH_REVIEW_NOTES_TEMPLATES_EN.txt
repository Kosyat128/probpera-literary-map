App overview
Literary Planet is a one-time paid educational literature app for adults
and families. It is built on the existing canonical Literary Planet of
probpera.ru and includes Russian and English application/content support.

Parent Center, Planetka, offline essentials and the Starter Set. There are
no subscriptions or ads. Optional non-consumable cosmetic additions are
not required.
