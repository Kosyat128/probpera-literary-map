# Bookshelf controls and dismissal

The primary empty-canvas click now returns either an inspected cover or an open book to the shelf. Pointer event capture records the gesture, so an out-and-back drag, cancelled pointer and Alt orbit do not become dismissal clicks. Leaving the canvas invalidates an uncaptured click candidate even when the pointer later returns to its starting point. Closing waits for the reconnected scene before restoring focus; the previous immediate focus targeted a node removed with the detail column.

The toolbar and collection rail use the site's violet palette, with orange lettering on warm-white buttons and white lettering on selected orange buttons. Search and quality each have a single focus frame, without a second bordered wrapper or glow. Controls share a 44px minimum height. The default editorial order no longer appears as a removable filter. Search, view, filters and collection management have stable responsive groups. Mobile collection actions fill their row; below 360px the quality label sits above the full-width selector so the longest Auto profile fits. Canonical book records and the preserved site Header/Hero are outside these changes.

Hover transitions take 140ms and presses 80ms, changing colour without moving buttons. Disabled actions remain visibly disabled. The filter drawer fades in over 160ms, supports Escape and returns focus to its trigger. Reduced motion removes the transitions and drawer animation.

Shelf and Catalogue form a matched pair, with consistent 18px icons and closer inner corners. Random and New shelf reuse the site's SVG icons. All three selectors use the same drawn chevron and restore the native arrow in forced-colour mode. The filter trigger exposes its expanded state and controls the actual dialog; quick filters retain accessible descriptions even when their compact visual labels are used.
