# Q: prepared media and native social transport

Current implementation checkpoint: 2026-09-27. The literary-news runner uses existing approved photographs first, then searches for a real, identity-matched author portrait when no approved asset exists. It never manufactures an illustration, event photograph or book cover. The same `prepareNewsPost` output is used by preview and delivery. Telegram uses multipart `sendPhoto` / `editMessageMedia`; its native caption is explicitly below the image. The implemented VK transport is deferred by the owner and remains off. Upload completion is not publication success. Implementation tests and the discovery proof below performed no real platform upload, message, post or production database write.

## Admission and cache

`data/news/social-media-assets.json` is the code-owned runtime registry. It now contains three approved archival portraits: Jason Reynolds (CC0), Edna St. Vincent Millay and George W. Bush (public domain). Their explicit permissions bind Telegram `-1002791579809` and VK `-231377018`; both code-owned destination modes are currently `off`. Telegram rights have been separately verified, but publication still requires reviewed history and a native canary. All VK activation is deferred. Existing source-article thumbnails are not treated as permission for social reuse.

Automatic discovery is deliberately narrower than all news topics. A reviewed writer identity must retain fresh Wikidata human identity; a new English headline search hint additionally requires an exact label/alias and a code-owned literary occupation. A hint alone never approves identity. An additional plausible person in the headline prevents selecting the sole person known to the local corpus. A unique P18 image must have an exact Commons file identity and supported rights. The resolver verifies Commons SHA-1 against the downloaded bytes, then records SHA-256, license evidence, attribution, transformations and the normalized derivative. Ambiguous identity, unsupported license or absent qualified portrait is explicitly held. Unknown or temporarily unavailable discovery stays pending before first delivery. No claim is made that every news item can receive an image.

Capture inspects at most eight news items and makes at most 24 actual HTTP requests per run, using fixed Wikimedia hosts, bounded bodies and deadlines. It persists semantic-revision cache records with CAS under `history:media:<revision>`: approved rights expire after 30 days, held results are retried after 14 days, and transient pending results after one hour. Never-checked items precede the oldest checked item, preventing repeated transient failures from starving the queue. Send consumes the durable cache and never searches during a provider write. Curated registry assets have priority. The metadata registry has a hard 5,000-asset limit with an explicit capacity failure, not silent eviction or 5,000 downloads.

Every admitted asset binds exact news IDs, subject/entity evidence, author/rightsholder, source URL and SHA-256, license evidence URL and SHA-256, checking method/date, expiry, transformation permissions, numeric destination permissions and the reviewed derivative descriptor. A string cannot substitute for the exact-ID or host arrays. Supported rights profiles are public domain, CC0, CC BY 4.0, ownership or explicit permission; NC/ND/SA are held. CC BY captions also carry the creator, license URL and a notice explaining orientation/resize/JPEG/metadata removal. Rights are rechecked at preparation and immediately before delivery, including destination binding and expiry. Network failures do not prove revocation.

Source downloads use the existing DNS-pinned HTTPS transport with a separate code-owned host allowlist, no redirects and all DNS answers required to be public. Input is capped at 8 MiB, 20 million pixels, one raster page and a 15-second fetch deadline. Only signature-matched JPEG/PNG/WebP is accepted; Sharp decodes the raster with a 10-second processing deadline. Output is contained within 1600×1600, never enlarged or cropped, converted to JPEG, limited to 2 MiB, and stripped of embedded metadata including GPS. Minimum side is 240 pixels. Hashes cover actual source and derivative bytes.

Derivatives are disposable local files in ignored `.tmp/news-social-media/<SHA256>.jpg`, outside JSONB and Git. A fresh runner preserves an approved photo descriptor even when its local JPEG is absent. Before send, it selects at most eight due photo news IDs and rebuilds only the pinned source and derivative hashes. Destination pause, mode, canary, history and cooldown are checked before this byte-preparation budget is spent. Failed reconstruction preserves the photo and remote identity, records an explicit reason and one-hour backoff with CAS, and prioritizes never-failed/oldest failures on later runs. This prevents a repeating first-48-failures cycle from blocking the tail. A concurrent remote receipt cannot be overwritten by an old reconstruction result. Transport consumes exact cached bytes and does not research or select images.

An explicit completed held search may use the full text variant with its recorded reason. Unsearched/pending discovery and a missing approved JPEG do not silently convert a photo into text. Expired announcements and withdrawals reach their existing resolution path even if discovery was pending. Telegram captions preserve the complete title, summary, date and mandatory credit; source and brand use native `text_link` entities rather than long printed URLs. `show_caption_above_media:false` is explicit. Captions that still exceed 1024 UTF-16 units use one complete text message with `required_credit_or_caption_exceeds_limit`; no facts or credit are truncated and no continuation message is invented.

## Upload, control and corrections

VK upload hosts have a separate provider allowlist (`pu[digits].vk.com`, `pu[digits].vkuserphoto.ru`, `pu[digits].userapi.com`, including `pu` without digits). Unknown hosts and redirects fail closed. POST transport pins validated public DNS answers, preserves Host/SNI, verifies TLS, has a 30-second deadline and limits replies to 256 KiB. Provider tokens are not sent to upload hosts. VK photos permission is checked as well as wall permission.

Native API calls also validate an explicit platform/method allowlist and fixed HTTPS hostname, with no credentials, port, query or fragment in the constructed endpoint. Telegram token characters cannot alter the endpoint path. Local injection tests prove rejected token shapes make zero requests; closure of the remote CodeQL alert still depends on the next CodeQL run. VK wall/photo methods require an authorized user token; no group-token shortcut is enabled.

Upload occurs before the publication dispatch marker. After it completes, current provider rights, pause/mode/canary/history, job version and destination controls are read again; the actual create/edit uses a fresh atomic SQL control fence. A pause, withdrawal, replacement revision or cooldown during upload prevents publication. Upload 429/auth errors update destination-wide cooldown/pause, including later jobs. An uncertain photo-send remains ambiguous and cannot create a text fallback or a new post.

The existing durable job stores a small `mediaCache` receipt scoped to platform, numeric destination, currently verified bot/user account ID and derivative SHA-256. VK's saved attachment receipt is committed with CAS **before** any wall publication can start. Telegram's returned `file_id` is saved only with an accepted message receipt. A restart, pause after upload or wall rate limit can reuse that exact resource, after current rights/expiry validation. A new account, destination or byte hash cannot reuse it; token renewal for the same verified account does not invalidate a still legal resource. No credential or image bytes enter JSONB. A crash before an upload receipt can be durably recorded can leave an orphaned file, but cannot create a duplicate post; unknown publication outcomes remain ambiguous.

Image/credit revisions preserve the durable job and known remote ID. Existing text posts are not automatically converted when the new photo profile becomes available. A profile-only restyle of an already sent photo is also preserved unless substantive content/asset information changes. Telegram photo-to-text removal (including a withdrawn/expired asset with no safe photo replacement) is explicitly blocked for operator resolution; the runner never deletes and recreates a post. VK can clear attachments with an edit. Platform-side editing remains subject to the existing preflight/canary gate.

The owner confirmed one new post per 30 minutes without catch-up bursts. The dispatcher now reserves a durable per-destination CAS slot immediately before its existing atomic dispatch fence. A crash or provider failure does not refund that slot, and corrections to known remote posts remain available. The 90-test integration receipt and independent pause/crash/unknown-result review are in `pacing-after-tests.json` and `pacing-independent-review.json`. A real paced canary still requires separate production evidence.

An admitted news item that disappears from the complete projection while its image search remains pending is explicitly held with `archived_media_requires_source_resolution`; its admission, identity and full prepared payload remain intact. Returning the same verified item resumes the search. The focused final correction has 46 passing tests and its own before/after evidence in `discovery/archived-media-resolution.json`.
